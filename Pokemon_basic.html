What is competitive Pokémon singles?

Competitive Pokémon singles is a form of competitive play that forces on 6v6 battles manly run by the fans.

Most of the community uses the Smogon tiering system and play on Pokémon showdown

usage tiers and there names

  • UBER, the highest tier of play, Pokémon banned of standard play go there
  • OU, standard play, stands for "Over Used"
  • UU, for Pokémon too weak for OU, stands for "Under Used"
  • RU, for Pokémon too weak for UU, stands for "Rarely Used
  • NU, for Pokémon too weak for RU, stands for "Never Used
  • PU, for Pokémon too weak NU, lowest official tier

The all tier are usage based (excluding UBER), Pokémon in a higher tier can't be used in a lower tier,

but you can a Pokémon form a lower tier in a higher tier.

Go to Pokémon Showdown to start play.
